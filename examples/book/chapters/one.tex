\centerline{\bf 1. Beginnings}
\bigskip

Every book starts with a blank page and an idea. This chapter is read with
{\tt\char`\\input chapters/one}, the way chapters of a long book are kept in
separate files, and rtex follows the input stack exactly as TeX does.

\def\lorem{Typesetting a book is mostly a matter of patience: words are set

other until the last word has found its place. }

algorithm, the glue between the words stretching or shrinking so that both
margins are straight. \lorem\lorem
