\centerline{\bf Hello from rtex}
\bigskip
This page was typeset by rtex, a TeX engine written in Rust. The mouth of the
engine---tokenizing, macro expansion, conditionals and assignments---is the
rtex-core crate; paragraphs, hyphenation, pages and the PDF come from rtex
itself, following Knuth's {\it TeX: The Program} line by line.

\def\greet#1{Hello, #1!}
Macros work as in any TeX: \greet{world} ``Quotes'' and dashes -- like these
--- become ligatures, and long words such as incomprehensibilities are
hyphenated with the patterns of {\tt hyphen.tex}.

Math is set as TeX sets it: $\sum_{k=1}^n k = {n(n+1)\over2}$ inline, and
$$\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = {\sqrt\pi\over2}. \eqno(1)$$
